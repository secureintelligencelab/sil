About the lab

A research group built around hard problems

We lead cybersecurity innovation through interdisciplinary research and close collaboration with industry, government, and academia.

The Secure Intelligence Lab is a research group at Crown Institute of Higher Education dedicated to advancing cybersecurity through novel applications of artificial intelligence and machine learning.

Our team of researchers, research assistants, and industry partners works on the security challenges of the digital age — building systems that protect critical infrastructure, personal data, and emerging technologies.

We work across threat detection, behavioural analytics, secure communication protocols, and adaptive AI-powered security systems that evolve alongside the threats they defend against.

  • AI-powered cybersecurityAdaptive defence systems
  • Threat & intrusion detectionIoV, IoMT, and network security
  • Machine & deep learningExplainable models
  • Blockchain technologyPrivacy-preserving systems
  • Behavioural analyticsKeystroke dynamics and identity
