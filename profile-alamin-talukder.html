Alamin Talukder

Research fellow focused on machine learning, deep learning, and their application to cybersecurity problems.

Biography

Alamin Talukder is a Research Fellow at the Secure Intelligence Lab. He holds an MSc in Computer Science and Engineering from Jagannath University, Bangladesh.

His research applies machine learning and deep learning techniques to cybersecurity challenges, including detection systems and intelligent threat analysis.

Research Interests

  • Machine learning
  • Deep learning
  • Applied cybersecurity
  • Threat detection systems

Role in the Lab

As Research Fellow, Alamin develops and evaluates machine-learning models that underpin several of the lab's detection and analysis projects.
