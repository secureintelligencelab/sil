Dr Ashraf Uddin

Manages the lab's research portfolio with a focus on blockchain, machine learning, and applied cybersecurity.

Biography

Dr Ashraf Uddin is the Research Manager of the Secure Intelligence Lab. He earned his PhD in IT (Cybersecurity) from Federation University Australia.

His work bridges blockchain technology, machine learning, and cybersecurity, with recent contributions to intrusion detection for the Internet of Vehicles and privacy-preserving blockchain systems for healthcare and academic records.

Research Interests

  • Blockchain and distributed ledger technology
  • Machine learning for security
  • Intrusion detection systems
  • Privacy-preserving systems and zero-knowledge proofs

Role in the Lab

As Research Manager, Ashraf coordinates the lab's active projects, manages collaborations, and serves as the primary point of contact for partnership enquiries.
